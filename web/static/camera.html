Meter reading

Aim at a neutral grey or white surface (grey card, white wall) under daylight or white LED light, and drag the box on it. Its brightness is not measured, only its colour matters. k comes from the camera's ISO rating (ISO 12232): about ±30 %, so fine for comparing cabs with the same k, not for absolute values. Studies on it are flagged. Empty fields use config/analysis.toml; a published saturation ISO for this body (measured ISO ÷ label) narrows the error.

Or type k

A typed k replaces the stored one, measured or not. It is kept in the history below with its note, and every study using it is flagged "k typed by hand".

Camera profile

A profile is one body, lens and aperture. Changing the lens means a new profile (copy, then calibrate): k does not carry over.

Lens geometry

Focal length, k1
—

Zero on the fiducial first (Movement page). The head then turns over a 5 × 5 grid around it (about ±24° pan, ±17° tilt) and fits focal length and k1 to where the fiducial lands. It rests on the gear ratios, and on live view showing the whole sensor. The head turns about its axes, not the lens: a near fiducial reads the focal length long by about pupil offset ÷ distance unless the offset is set in mount.toml [optical].
